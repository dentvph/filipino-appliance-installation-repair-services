Important Safety Guidelines

Prevention First

  • Regular maintenance prevents major issues
  • Follow manufacturer guidelines
  • Keep manuals accessible

When to Call a Pro

  • Electrical issues or burning smell
  • Gas leaks or unusual odors
  • Major water leaks

Safety First

  • Never attempt electrical repairs
  • Don't remove safety features
  • Avoid DIY gas appliance work

Critical Safety Warning

These guides are for basic troubleshooting only. DO NOT attempt repairs if:

  • You smell gas or burning
  • There's electrical sparking or exposed wires
  • The appliance is under warranty
  • You lack proper tools or safety equipment
  • You're unsure about any step

Improper repairs can lead to:

  • Fire hazards and electrical risks
  • Permanent appliance damage
  • Voided warranties
  • Higher repair costs
  • Personal injury
